About TabTin

Our Mission

TabTin is a free community-driven platform dedicated to combating phone theft. We provide a simple, accessible database where stolen phones can be reported and verified by anyone — buyers, sellers, repair technicians, and law enforcement.

How It Works

For Victims: Report your stolen phone with its IMEI number. Your device is flagged instantly. If someone finds it and searches the IMEI, they can contact you securely through TabTin without ever seeing your personal details.

For Buyers & Engineers: Before purchasing or repairing a phone, search the IMEI on TabTin. If stolen, you can alert the owner with one click.

What is an IMEI?

IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) is a unique 15-digit number for every mobile phone. Find yours by dialing *#06# or in Settings → About Phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is TabTin?▼
TabTin is a free online platform where people can report stolen phones and check if a phone they want to buy has been reported stolen. It serves as a community-driven stolen phone registry.
How do I find my IMEI number?▼
Dial *#06# on your phone — the IMEI will appear on screen. You can also find it in Settings → About Phone, or on the original box. It's usually a 15-digit number.
Is TabTin free?▼
Yes, completely free. No charges for reporting or searching. Our mission is community protection.
Can someone contact me if they find my phone?▼
Yes! When someone searches your stolen phone's IMEI, they'll see a "Contact Owner" button. They fill out a form, and TabTin sends you an email with their details — your contact info stays hidden from them.
Is my contact information visible to searchers?▼
No. Searchers only see your masked email (e.g. jo***@gmail.com) and masked phone. They can only reach you through TabTin's secure contact form. Your full details are never exposed.
Can I remove a report if I recover my phone?▼
Yes. Use the Contact Us page to request removal. Provide your Report ID and proof of ownership, and we'll remove it promptly.
What if a phone is falsely reported?▼
Contact us with proof of purchase/ownership. We investigate all claims and remove false reports. Filing false reports is strictly prohibited.
Who can use TabTin?▼
Anyone — individuals, phone repair technicians, second-hand dealers, and law enforcement.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When reporting: IMEI, phone details, your email and optionally phone number. When searching: only the IMEI query (not stored). When contacting an owner: your name, email, and message.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To maintain the stolen phone database
  • To notify you when someone finds your phone
  • To facilitate secure communication between finders and owners
  • To respond to Contact Us inquiries

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information. Reporter contact details are never shown to searchers. IMEI numbers are partially masked in results. When a finder contacts an owner, the finder's details are shared with the owner via email so they can coordinate — but the owner's info remains hidden from the finder.

4. Data Security

Data is stored securely using Google's infrastructure with appropriate protections against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention

Reports remain until removal is requested. Contact messages are retained for support purposes.

6. Your Rights

Request access, correction, or deletion of your data via Contact Us with your Report ID.

7. Cookies

TabTin does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

8. Changes

Updates will be posted here with a new effective date.
